Delete your Indian Taash account

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Indian Taash and sign in.
  2. Open your Profile (the profile button) and choose Delete account.
  3. Confirm. Your account is deleted at once.

If the app asks you to sign in again first, sign in with your phone number once more and choose Delete account again. This is a security step required by our sign-in provider.

If you cannot open the app

Email [OWNER: support email] from any address with the subject "Delete my account", and include the mobile number you signed up with and your username. We will verify ownership (for example by sending a one-time code to that number) and delete the account within [OWNER: e.g. 30] days, then confirm by email.

What is deleted

  • Your phone number, name, language and sign-up record
  • Your profile, username and avatar
  • Your online status
  • Your friends, friend requests, blocks, pokes and play requests
  • Your login (Firebase Authentication)

What may be kept

  • Safety reports that other players filed about your account, kept only as long as needed to protect others [OWNER: state period].
  • Online table data is deleted automatically about six hours after a table is created.
  • Records on your own device are removed when you uninstall the app.
  • Data that Google holds under its own policies (for example Google Play purchase history and advertising data).
