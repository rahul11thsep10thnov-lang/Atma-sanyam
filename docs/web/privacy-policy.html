What the app is

Indian Taash is a card and board games app (points only — there is no real-money play, prize or cash-out). You can play alone against the computer without an account.

Sharing

Other players see your username and avatar, and your online status if you have not chosen to appear offline. Your phone number is never shown to other players. We share data with Google (Firebase, AdMob, Google Play) as service providers. We do not sell your data.

Your choices and deleting your account

In the app: Profile → Delete account permanently deletes your phone number and name, profile, username, picture, online status, friend records, pokes, play requests and your login. You can also request deletion on the web at the deletion page. Safety reports about an account may be kept to protect other players until they are reviewed. Advertising data held by Google is governed by Google's policies; you can reset your advertising ID in Android settings.
