# Design decision log — Friends & social (and the board games to follow)

Every element below was presented as a gallery of ten genuinely different designs with one
recommendation, and nothing was built until the owner approved a design. The approved design is the
reference for everything that touches that element; later elements reuse its choices (the royal
court theme: antique wood, gold bevels, parchment text, Cinzel/Marcellus-style headings).

| # | Stage | Approved | Notes that bind later work |
|---|-------|----------|----------------------------|
| 1 | Ludo tile in the Entertainment tab | Design 1 (Royal court, the Khel tile) | Gold-framed portrait (cross board on ivory, two tokens, a die), crimson plaque, one-clause summary, 2–4 players; tapping opens Ludo's own introduction page. Entertainment uses Khel's two-column grid. |
| 2 | Introduction and mode selection | Design 1 (the card games' setup page) | Portrait + two-line intro + Rules; mode slots; Players 2–4; AI difficulty; House rules plaque with orb switches preset to the recommended ruleset (six to enter, extra roll on six with three-six forfeit, extra roll on capture, exact finish, play on for places; star squares safe). The engine implements exactly these switches. |
| 3 | Board | Design 10 (Royal court, the card room's board) | Ivory on waxed walnut with a gold rim; suit-coloured yards with ivory nests; gold safe stars; coloured home columns; four-colour star under a gold medallion. Tokens carry suit symbols; stacks show counts; die and Roll below the board. Geometry shared by renderer and engine. |
| 4 | Tokens, dice, safe squares, homes, movement | Design 3 (Glide) | Die spins through faces and settles (gold flash on a six); tokens glide along an arc with a fading trail; captured tokens fly home shrinking; safe squares shine on landing; engine decides first, taps ignored and bots wait while moving; reduced motion snaps. |
| 5 | Avatars, usernames, turn indicators, scores | Design 1, modified: each plaque in its own yard | Avatar with colour ring and suit badge, username, four gold token pips, glow on the current player, place when finished; plaque centred in the player's yard between the nests; no row above the board. |
| 6 | Lobby and room creation | Design 1 (the card games' lobby, coloured per seat) | The same lobby page, controller and transports as the card games; one row per seat tinted in its yard colour (host red, first guest opposite in yellow), open seats listed as the computer's; a house-rules line under the room code, rebuilt from what the host announces. House rules travel as five flags in `MatchConfig.options` / the LOBBY and START messages; the room document records `seats` and `options`. Ludo joins `GameId` (not the card catalog) so rooms, presence and play requests can carry it. The table runs `LudoMachine` on the lockstep session; the host rolls from the step's shared random. |
| 7 | Friends invitation and play requests | Design 1 (the Friends play request, plus Invite friends in the lobby) | The existing play-request card, waiting banner, bell entry and profile game picker learn Ludo through one `GameNames` lookup (card and board catalogs); accepting opens the board lobby at the room; a profile request with no table makes the sender host Ludo with their last setup. The Ludo lobby's host gets an Invite friends button (online rooms, while seats are open) opening a sheet of friends ranked online-and-free first; each Invite sends a play request carrying the room code. Same request rules as the card games (friends only, both free, one pending per pair, 60 s). |
| 8 | Waiting, reconnecting, game-full states | Design 1 (plaque states and parchment bands) | A dropped player's seat is kept 60 s (`SeatKeeper`): the computer plays it at once (BOT) and everyone is told (AWAY); their yard plaque dims with an hourglass and "back in N s", then shows the robot disc and "Computer". The dropped phone shows a band over the frozen board, re-sends JOIN every 3 s, and is reseated with the full position (SEAT, now allowed for a seated guest); "You're back!" for a moment. Online rooms rejoin by themselves (same uid); Nearby rejoins through the lobby by name. A full lobby offers Watch (JOIN with the watch flag): the late arrival sees the table fill and is admitted as a watcher at the start. Host leaving still ends the table; no turn timer. The band and dialog logic (`SessionNotices`) also serves the card tables. |
| 9 | Quick messages, reactions, sound controls | Design 1 (talk button by the die, bubbles at the yard) | The Element 11 talk button and tray sit in the die row; a message shows as a parchment bubble under the speaker's plaque (above it in the bottom yards), a watcher's over the centre; the header speaker is the existing sound panel; mute per seat stays in the tray. The 20 board-pack sounds are in `res/raw` as `sfx_*`; `LudoSfx` maps each engine event once (die spin → land → bonus on a six, enter / move / step ticks / capture / near finish / home, your turn, forfeit and refused taps, match complete with victory or defeat per phone) through the same per-step hook as the card games (`GameMachine.sfxFor` adds the listener's seat); the solo table uses the same mapping. Table-talk arrival now uses soft_notification at every table. |
| 10 | Results, statistics, rematch, return to lobby | Design 5 (statistics table) | The end-of-match dialog is a table — place, turns, sixes, captures, lost, home — one row per player in finishing order (your row gold), your lifetime record under it (played · won · second · third), then Rematch (host) / Play again (solo) / "Waiting for the host…" (guests, watchers) and Back to Khel. The engine counts the statistics (`LudoStats` in the state, in the codec) and ranks the unfinished players by tokens home, then progress, when the match ends early. Rematch is a lockstep move (`LudoAction.Rematch`, "N") only the host may make and only when the match is over; it keeps names, colours, computer seats and rules. The record is kept per account on the phone (`LudoRecordRepository`) and shown on My Profile as "Ludo: N played · M won". |

### Second round (owner's follow-up)

- Cards: exactly like enclosures 2 and 3 — classic white deck, suit symbol under the rank, rank 20% larger, traditional court figures, red lattice back; gold trim removed.
- Settings gains Screen orientation: Portrait / Landscape / Auto with phone icons.
- Landscape tables are true full screen (no header or game name, no app bar, banner or system bars).
- Back always asks "Leave the game?" Yes / No; the setup page no longer auto-opens Rules.
- Ludo / Moksha Patam portrait fills the table (big die row on tall phones); own colour is chosen on setup and always drawn bottom-left.

### Third round (printed Ludo board, matte gotis, branding)

- Ludo: the flat printed board of the supplied picture (colours FF1018 / 25E500 / 55C7F5 / FFF500, thin black grid), 2 × 2 yard circles at ±0.9 cell from each yard's centre, coloured start cells, four coloured stars on white, coloured entry arrows with black outlines, centre triangles with black dividers. Square, not stretched. Rules and path logic are untouched.
- Gotis: Design 5 (matte), no gloss, soft contact shadow.
- First tab renamed Cards; app icon and opening picture are the supplied artwork.
