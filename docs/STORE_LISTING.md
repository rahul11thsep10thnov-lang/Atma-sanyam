# Google Play store listing — version 1 (offline)

Copy these into Play Console → *Grow users → Store presence → Main store listing*. English is the
default language; add Hindi under *Manage translations → Add your own translations → Hindi (hi-IN)*.

The texts describe **only what version 1 does**: it works offline, has no accounts, no friends,
no ads and no purchases. Don't add features that are hidden in this build (friends, group focus,
membership), because Google rejects listings that promise things the app doesn't do.

Google's limits: app name ≤ 30 characters, short description ≤ 80, full description ≤ 4,000.
The counts below were checked. Don't use words like "best", "#1" or "free download", and don't
put emoji in the name: Google rejects them.

---

## English (en-IN / en-US)

**App name** (26 characters)

```
Tame: Focus Timer & Garden
```

**Short description** (79 characters)

```
Stay off your phone, grow plants and reveal jigsaws. Offline, no ads, no login.
```

**Full description** (about 1,780 characters)

```
Tame helps you put the phone down and get real work done. Start a focus session and your time turns into something you can see: a plant that grows beside you, or a picture that reveals itself tile by tile.

HOW IT WORKS
• Pick a plant or a picture, set the timer by turning the dial or typing the minutes, and press Start.
• Stay in Tame while you focus. Leave the app and you have a few seconds to come back; stay away and the session fails, leaving a wilted sapling or a broken picture.
• Finish the session and your plant grows, your picture is revealed, and you earn one coin for every focused minute.

GROW YOUR OWN PLACES
• Balcony: fill your planters with the plants you grow.
• Paradise Garden: a 3D garden you can walk around, turn and zoom.
• My Museum: hang the jigsaws you complete and arrange your collection.
• Store: spend your coins on decorations and furniture for each place.

JIGSAWS FROM REAL PICTURES
• A built-in library of nature, wildlife and inspirational pictures.
• Or pick a photo of your own: it stays on your phone.
• The longer you focus, the bigger the jigsaw.

SEE YOUR PROGRESS
• Total focus time, day streaks, longest session and this week at a glance.
• Every session leaves a picture in your history.

MADE TO BE CALM
• Works completely offline. No account, no sign-in.
• No ads and no in-app purchases.
• Nothing leaves your phone: your history, plants, coins and photos stay on your device.
• Morning and night themes, gentle motion, sound and haptics you can turn off.
• Optional reminder if you leave the app in the middle of a session.
• In English and Hindi.

Tame is for students preparing for exams, people working from home, and anyone who picks up the phone more often than they would like. Tame your time, one session at a time.
```
